Buying & selling guide +

Auction House

  • /ah — Open the auction house and browse player listings.
  • /ah sell <price> [amt] — List the item in your hand.
  • /ah search <keyword> — Search auction listings.
  • /ah cancel <id> — Cancel one of your active listings.
  • /ah active — View your current listings.
  • /ah claim — Claim sold, expired or purchased items.

General economy

  • /shop — Open the server shop menu.
  • /shop sell — Open the sell menu.
  • /sell — Sell the item in your hand.
  • /sell all — Sell items from your inventory.
  • /balance — Check your balance.
  • /pay <name> <amount> — Pay another player.
  • /withdraw <amount> — Withdraw banknotes.
  • /deposit — Deposit banknotes.
TPA guide & costs +

Core commands

  • /tpa <player> — Request teleport to a player.
  • /tpahere <player> — Request a player teleport to you.
  • /tpaccept / /tpadeny — Accept or deny requests.
  • /tpatoggle — Turn requests on or off.
  • /tpablock <player> / /tpaunblock <player> — Manage blocked requests.
  • /tphome set|list|delete|default|name — Manage homes.
  • /rtp — Random teleport in your current realm.
  • /spawn — Return to spawn.
  • /tpaback — Return to where you last died.

Costs

Ominous chest lock guide +

Locking

  • Lock chests, barrels and shulkers to a named trial or ominous trial key.
  • Rename the key in an anvil, then right-click a container while holding it.
  • Only matching key-name holders can open, break or interact.
  • Locks are key-name based, not player based.
  • A key name can lock one container at a time.
  • Non-ominous keys lock the container once.

Lockpicking

  • Rusty picks use copper, a tripwire hook and a stick.
  • Normal picks use iron, a tripwire hook and a breeze rod.
  • Silence picks use a normal pick, silence trim and an echo shard.
  • The pin-depth puzzle costs one attempt per TURN LOCK.
  • Failures deal damage and can trigger lockouts.
  • Only one active picker can work on a container at once.
